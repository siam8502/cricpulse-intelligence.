# Fixture sources

Primary: [ICC fixtures and results](https://www.icc-cricket.com/fixtures-results?appview=true)

Cross-check: [Cricbuzz series schedule](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-schedule/series)

Historical ball-by-ball: [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org/). Cricsheet is not a live fixture feed.

The project does not assume an unrestricted free Cricbuzz API. Verify fixture times and timezone at the source. Do not scrape undocumented/private endpoints.
